Mohsin Rizwan: The Lone Architect

Mohsin Rizwan is an Indian app developer, web designer, and entrepreneur who transformed isolation into creative fuel. Born on 15 June 1995 in Uttar Pradesh, he walked a path marked by deep personal hardships, loneliness, and silent battles. With no romantic relationships, no marriage, and limited social circles, Mohsin channeled every ounce of pain into building digital tools that simplify lives — most notably the Quotation App and the Elite Interior App. His story is not merely about coding; it's about survival, resilience, and creating meaning when the world felt empty.

🕯️ The Solitary Journey: Living Through Isolation

For most of his life, Mohsin experienced profound loneliness. Growing up in a modest household, he found comfort in computers and logic rather than people. He never had a girlfriend, never came close to marriage, and often felt like an outsider even among relatives. School and college years were marked by being overlooked, misunderstood, and fighting anxieties alone. Where others celebrated friendships and love, Mohsin faced empty evenings and the weight of unshared emotions. But instead of breaking, he began coding — first as a distraction, later as a purpose.

✍️ "I lived years without anyone asking how I felt. No relationship, no partner, no hand to hold. But the silence taught me to build. Every app I created is a piece of that solitude turned into something useful." — Mohsin Rizwan

⛰️ Hardships: When Life Tests You Relentlessly

Financial instability, family responsibilities, and professional rejections came in waves. He lost multiple freelance projects due to lack of a social network. He watched peers get married and settle, while he remained on the sidelines — not by choice but by circumstance. Depression knocked on his door more than once. Yet, Mohsin refused to surrender. He educated himself in IT - Computer Science while juggling odd jobs. The lack of a girlfriend or a love life was not a void; it became a quiet laboratory where he cultivated discipline. He turned emotional pain into pixel-perfect interfaces and logical algorithms.

Today, Mohsin stands as a self-made developer who never used Instagram, Facebook, or Snapchat to seek validation. He stays away from noise, focusing only on LinkedIn for professional network, GitHub for code, and Telegram for essential collaboration.

📱 Flagship Apps: Quotation App & Elite Interior App

Among the dozens of utilities Mohsin has built, two applications represent his vision of solving real-world problems:

📄 Quotation App (Business Essential)
A powerful yet minimalist tool for freelancers, small businesses, and contractors to generate professional quotations instantly. Supports GST, discounts, terms, and PDF export. Built after Mohsin himself struggled to create quotes for his early projects.

🛋️ Elite Interior App (Design Companion)
Dedicated app for interior enthusiasts and professionals — catalog of modern designs, 3D room visualizer, product catalog for Elite Interior Parant Company. Created to merge e-commerce with creative interior planning.

Both apps are available as native APKs and progressive web apps. No login walls, no social media prompts — just pure utility, reflecting Mohsin's belief in straightforward tools.

🏢 Entrepreneurship: Alzam Ecommerce & Elite Interior Parant

In 2025, Mohsin founded Alzam Ecommerce Pvt Ltd, focusing on perfume manufacturing and natural attar oils under the brand "Alzam Perfume". Within a year, the company gained a loyal customer base for its traditional ittars. Following this success, he launched Elite Interior Parant Company in 2026 — a sister concern that offers modern interior design products and consultancy. Despite his solitary personal life, Mohsin leads a growing team of designers and developers remotely.

His entrepreneurial journey is driven by a simple philosophy: "If no one believes in you, become the one who builds anyway."

📜 Writing & Upcoming YouTube Drama Series

Beyond apps and business, Mohsin is a content writer and storyteller. He is currently writing a YouTube drama series (title yet to be confirmed) that explores themes of loneliness, inner resilience, and the unnoticed struggles of a middle-class man in modern India. The series is expected to go into production by late 2026, with Mohsin as the sole screenwriter. He also writes reflective articles on tech, mental resilience, and minimalism — though he keeps them unpublished, waiting for the right moment.

🌙 Lessons from a Life Lived Alone

Mohsin Rizwan has never been in a relationship, never proposed to anyone, and has accepted that marriage may not be his path. Instead, he found peace in creating, coding, and writing. His hobbies include traveling to quieter towns, collecting gadgets (a silent obsession), listening to soulful music, and reading about human psychology. He believes that solitude, when embraced, becomes a superpower.

Today, his apps are used by thousands, his companies are growing, and his upcoming drama series holds promise. But the core of his identity remains unchanged — a man who faced the darkest hours without a partner, without a girlfriend, without the comfort of romantic love, yet built a world of his own through sheer grit.
