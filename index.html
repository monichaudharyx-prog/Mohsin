Mohsin Rizwan

Mohsin Rizwan (born 15 June 1995) is an Indian app developer, web designer, content writer, and entrepreneur. He is the creator of Quotation App and Elite Interior App, and the founder of Alzam Ecommerce Pvt Ltd (2025) and Elite Interior Parant Company (2026). His life is marked by prolonged solitude, emotional hardship, and a complete absence of romantic relationships — a reality that deeply shaped his work and worldview. Mohsin does not use mainstream social media (no Facebook, Instagram, or Snapchat), preferring only LinkedIn, GitHub, and Telegram for professional communication.

Early life and family

Mohsin was born in Uttar Pradesh, India to Rizwan Shahid and Munazari Begum. He grew up with his elder brother Nazim Chaudhary and younger brother Amir Chaudhary. From a young age, he felt different from his peers — he found comfort in academic subjects, computers, and writing rather than social gatherings. He earned a degree in IT - Computer Science but struggled to fit into conventional social structures. While classmates built friendships and relationships, Mohsin experienced deepening isolation. This isolation soon transformed into a driver for self-education in web and app development.

Solitary path: No girlfriend, no marriage — a life of resilience

Throughout his life, Mohsin has never been in a romantic relationship. He never had a girlfriend, and the prospect of marriage never materialized due to a combination of introversion, life circumstances, and a focus on survival. He watched relatives and peers marry, celebrate engagements, and build families while he stood on the margins: alone, mostly unheard, and often misunderstood. The absence of a partner became a powerful silence — one that he filled with code, literature, and app development.

“I spent years without anyone asking how I was. No girlfriend, no marriage, no hand to hold. That loneliness could have destroyed me. Instead, I built apps — for people who needed tools, not sympathy.”
— Mohsin Rizwan

Financial instability, family obligations, and career rejections added layers of hardship. Yet Mohsin refused to break. He taught himself advanced programming, UI/UX design, and content writing, working as a freelancer in the shadows. The lack of a love life never became bitterness; it became a quiet workshop where every utility tool — the Quotation App, the Elite Interior App — was forged from lived experience of building something from nothing.

Notable apps: Quotation App & Elite Interior App

Among his many creations, two applications stand out as flagship utilities, both designed to address concrete business needs without unnecessary complexity.

📄 Quotation App
A minimalist quotation generator for freelancers, small businesses, and contractors. Supports itemized lists, GST calculations, discount logic, and PDF export. Born from Mohsin's own struggle to create professional quotes during his freelance days.

🛋️ Elite Interior App

Both apps are available as native Android APKs and progressive web apps. No login gates, no social media integration — designed for straightforward offline-first usage, reflecting Mohsin's own preference for quiet, functional software.

Entrepreneurship: Alzam Ecommerce & Elite Interior

In 2025, Mohsin founded Alzam Ecommerce Pvt Ltd to manufacture and sell traditional attar perfumes under the "Alzam Perfume" label. The company quickly gained traction for its authentic ittars and affordable luxury. In 2026, he expanded into the home improvement sector by launching Elite Interior Parant Company — a sister venture focused on modern interior products, modular furniture, and design consultation. Despite his solitary personal life, Mohsin leads a lean team of designers and support staff, managing operations remotely.

Writing & upcoming drama series

Beyond apps and business, Mohsin is a content writer and scriptwriter. He is currently writing a YouTube drama series (title yet to be confirmed) that explores themes of loneliness, unspoken male trauma, and the resilience of a common man in urban India. The series is expected to begin production in late 2026, with Mohsin as the sole scriptwriter and creative director. He also writes reflective essays on minimalism, mental health, and technology — though most remain unpublished, reserved for the right moment.

Personal ethos, hobbies and daily life

Mohsin has never used Facebook, Instagram, or Snapchat. He avoids all entertainment-oriented social networks, considering them distractions. His professional presence exists only on LinkedIn (for work), GitHub (code repositories), and Telegram (tech discussions). He spends his free time traveling to non-touristy destinations, exploring new gadgets (gadget buying is a quiet hobby), listening to a wide range of music — from classical to ambient — and reading about human psychology. He has accepted that marriage may not be part of his future. Instead, he finds peace in building tools that serve others, one line of code at a time.

His life is a testament to the idea that a man can be entirely alone — no girlfriend, no wife, no romantic companionship — and still create meaning. Mohsin Rizwan continues to develop utility apps, manage his companies, and write his drama series, proving that solitude can fuel extraordinary craftsmanship.
