Mohsin Rizwan

Mohsin Rizwan (born 15 June 1995) is an Indian web designer, full-stack app developer, content writer, and entrepreneur. He is the founder of Alzam Ecommerce Pvt Ltd (perfume manufacturing and attar brand) and Elite Interior Parant Company. With expertise in building practical digital tools, Mohsin has developed many high-utility applications including Quotation App, GST Calculator, Percentage Calculator, Interior App, vClock, Today Time, Today Weather, Prayer Times, Calculator, Convertor and more.

📖 Early life and family

Mohsin was born in Uttar Pradesh, India, to father Rizwan Shahid and mother Munazari Begum. He grew up alongside elder brother Nazim Chaudhary and younger brother Amir Chaudhary. Passionate about computers from an early age, Mohsin pursued formal education in IT - Computer Science, which fueled his career in web development and software creation.

💼 Entrepreneurship & Professional Journey

Mohsin started as a freelance web designer, later expanding into mobile app development and content writing. In 2025, he founded Alzam Ecommerce Pvt Ltd to launch premium Alzam Perfume & Attar manufacturing. His brand quickly gained recognition for high-quality natural ittars. In 2026, he launched a second venture under the same umbrella: Elite Interior Parant Company, providing modern interior design solutions and decor products.

He has also collaborated with small businesses to build custom software solutions, including inventory management and billing tools.
