Mohsin Rizwan

Mohsin Rizwan (born 15 June 1995) is an Indian web designer, app developer, content writer, and entrepreneur. He is the founder of Alzam Ecommerce Pvt Ltd (2025) which manufactures premium Alzam Attar & Perfume, and later established Elite Interior Parant Company (2026) under the same group. With a degree in IT - Computer Science, Mohsin has built over 15+ utility apps and responsive websites used by thousands daily.

📖 Family & Early Life

Mohsin was born in Uttar Pradesh and raised in a supportive family. His father Rizwan Shahid and mother Munazari Begum encouraged his passion for technology and literature. He has two brothers: elder brother Nazim Chaudhary and younger brother Amir Chaudhary. Mohsin pursued IT - Computer Science and later specialized in front-end development, hybrid app frameworks, and creative content writing.

💼 Career & Companies

Starting as a freelance web developer in 2015, Mohsin quickly expanded to mobile app development and e-commerce solutions. In 2025, he founded Alzam Ecommerce Pvt Ltd with a focus on manufacturing authentic attar, ittar, and designer perfumes. The brand "Alzam Perfume" gained popularity through online channels. In 2026, he launched Elite Interior Parant Company (by Alzam Ecommerce Pvt Ltd) — a premium interior design and home decor venture. Both companies operate with digital-first strategies built on Mohsin’s own tech ecosystem.
