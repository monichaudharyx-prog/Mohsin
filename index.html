Mohsin Rizwan

Mohsin Rizwan (born 15 June 1995) is an Indian web designer, app developer, content writer, and entrepreneur. He is the founder of Alzam Ecommerce Pvt Ltd (2025) and Elite Interior Parant Company under Alzam Group. With a background in IT and computer science, Mohsin has built a reputation for crafting modern websites, utility apps, and original digital content.

📖 Early life and family

Mohsin Rizwan was born in Uttar Pradesh, India. He is the son of Rizwan Shahid and Munazari Begum. He has two brothers: elder brother Nazim Chaudhary and younger brother Amir Chaudhary. From an early age, Mohsin showed a deep interest in computers and creative writing. He pursued formal education in IT - Computer Science, which laid the foundation for his career in web and app development.

💼 Career & Entrepreneurship

Mohsin started his professional journey as a freelance web designer and gradually expanded into app development and content strategy. In 2025, he founded Alzam Ecommerce Pvt Ltd, a company focused on perfume manufacturing and e-commerce under the brand Alzam Perfume & Attar. The same year, his flagship brand Alzam Attar gained traction in the fragrance industry for its high-quality natural ittars.

In 2026, Mohsin launched a sister venture — Elite Interior Parant Company (by Alzam Ecommerce Pvt Ltd), offering premium interior solutions and modern decorative products. Under his leadership, both companies are scaling their digital presence using innovative tools built by his own development team.
